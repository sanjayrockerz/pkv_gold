## Brand & Style

This design system establishes the visual language for a modern luxury bullion house and fine jewellery valuation atelier. It synthesizes heirloom Indian heritage with disciplined, contemporary European editorial art direction. The atmosphere is that of an exclusive private vault or an archival museum floor: dimly lit, deliberate, and intensely focused on the physical materiality of precious metals.

The visual style merges **Quiet Luxury** with **Precision Instrumentation**. It deliberately avoids decorative clichés, celebratory gaudiness, fluorescent saturation, and hyperactive software paradigms. Visual hierarchy is established through extreme contrast in typographic scale, vast negative space, tactile hairline separation, and the selective gleam of directional light skimming over burnished gold.

## Colors

The palette draws entirely from physical matter: dark volcanic stone, aged espresso wood, unlacquered metals, and heavy rag paper.

- **Base Environments:**
  - `Canvas / Abyss`: `#080807` (Dominant page ground, absolute void)
  - `Vault Surface`: `#0F0E0B` (Primary paneling and card ground)
  - `Elevated Bed`: `#15130F` (Active surfaces, raised instrument wells)
  - `Recessed Well`: `#0A0908` (Input fields, recessed analytical trays)

- **Precious Accents (The Gold Standard):**
  - `Reflective Gold`: `#E4C979` (Specular highlights, focus rings, fine micro-accents)
  - `Primary Sovereign Gold`: `#D4AF55` (Key actions, active toggles, primary monetary tallies)
  - `Burnished Gold`: `#B89132` (Structural borders, subtle status indicators, inactive metal states)
  - `Muted Antique Patina`: `#6E5624` (Subtle boundary lines, inactive toggles, divider rules)

- **Typography & Architectural Grays:**
  - `Warm Ivory`: `#F4EFE5` (Headlines, primary balance valuations, hero figures)
  - `Muted Alabaster`: `#D1CBC0` (Body copy, narrative descriptions)
  - `Weathered Stone / Taupe`: `#9C978C` (Secondary metadata, field labels, axis markers)
  - `Submerged Carbon`: `#4A463F` (Disabled items, ghost rules, subtle grid axes)

Color is applied with severe restraint. Gold is never sprayed across wide decorative backgrounds or used as sweeping gradients; it is preserved strictly for precise focal points, fine hairline strokes, tactile indicators, and real-time valuation metrics.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional tension between classical, high-contrast serif romanticism and cold, technical analytical precision.

- **Editorial Headings (`Bodoni Moda`):** Used for curatorial statements, archive titles, and experiential intros. Features razor-thin serifs and vertical stress reminiscent of bespoke print monographs.
- **Literary Narrative (`EB Garamond`):** Used for editorial narratives, historical context of provenance, and provenance notes. Delivers an unhurried, bookish reading cadence.
- **Instrument Monospace & Metrics (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Applied to all functional touchpoints: karat purity, live weight readouts, spot prices, ledger entries, and interactive inputs. 
- **Micro-Labels & Metadata (`label-caps-wide`):** Always rendered in uppercase with aggressive tracking (`0.18em`) to mimic stamped hallmarks on ingot surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored to a 12-column architectural grid with wide exterior margins that frame screens like gallery mats.

- **Grid Architecture:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12 columns, 2rem gutters, 5rem outer margins. Max content container width: 1440px.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 1.5rem gutters, 3rem outer margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem gutters, 1.25rem outer margins.

- **Compositional Rhythm:**
  Generous vertical negative space creates visual gravity. Hero sections and valuation showcases require deep separation (`unit-4xl` to `unit-5xl`), allowing singular objects or figures to command the canvas. Dense information modules—such as live weight and karat calculation matrices—shift to compact, precision tabular layouts using rigid borders instead of floating spatial gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating, cloud-like drop shadows. It relies on architectural planar nesting, dark tonal layering, and fine light catchers.

- **Tonal Stepping:**
  - Ground floor: `#080807`
  - Mid-plane (Containers, inspection cards): `#0F0E0B`
  - High-plane (Floating valuation consoles, sheet drawers): `#15130F`

- **Hairline Framing & Ghost Edges:**
  Boundaries are articulated through ultra-fine 1px borders colored in `#211E18` (dormant state) or `#3A3428` (interactive state).

- **Directional Specular Lighting:**
  Rather than ambient elevation shadows, active cards and interactive elements simulate physical light falloff. Focused active modules feature an internal top-edge hairline (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(228, 201, 121, 0.25)`), evoking the glint along the bevel of a gold ingot held under a directional gallery spotlight.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly sharp (`roundedness: 0`). Elements have 0px border-radius across all breakpoints.

Sharp, rectilinear architecture reinforces the visual metaphor of cut bullion, precision-milled gold bars, architectural vaults, and fine-gauge metal calipers. Curved, bubbly, or pill-shaped geometries are strictly forbidden to ensure the experience remains grounded in high-jewelry rigor, structural authority, and discipline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign:** Sharp rectangular block. Solid `#D4AF55` background with `#080807` bold grotesque text in wide caps (`label-caps-compact`). Hover state transitions background smoothly to `#E4C979` with a delicate golden bloom.
- **Secondary Architectural:** Transparent background, 1px solid border in `#6E5624`, text in `#F4EFE5`. Hover state elevates the border to `#D4AF55` and adds a subtle backdrop tint of `rgba(212, 175, 85, 0.04)`.
- **Text Link / Action:** Typographic-only with an offset 1px underline. Underline sits 4px below text and spans only the width of the label. Color: `#9C978C`, transitioning to `#D4AF55` on hover.

### Inputs & Valuation Calculator Controls
- **Numeric Fields & Scales:** Sharp container with background `#0A0908` and a 1px border in `#211E18`. Text is right-aligned in `metric-body` (`Hanken Grotesk`) in `#F4EFE5`.
- **Labels:** Pinned to the top-left corner in uppercase tracked mono-sans (`label-caps-wide`), rendered in `#9C978C`.
- **Focus State:** 1px crisp outline in `#D4AF55` accompanied by an inset specular edge: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(228, 201, 121, 0.3)`. No generic browser rings or neon blooms.

### Cards & Valuation Trays
- **Exhibition Plinth:** Background `#0F0E0B`, bounded by 1px solid `#1A1814`. Zero corner radius. Card interiors are divided using hairline rules rather than floating cards inside cards.
- **Interactive State:** Hover subtly lifts the border to `#3A3428`, illuminating a micro-tag in the top-right indicating purity (`e.g., 999.9 FINE GOLD`).

### Chips & Hallmarks
- **Karat / Purity Tags:** Rectilinear boxes, padding `4px 10px`, zero border radius.
- **Styling:** Border 1px solid `#3A3428`, background `rgba(21, 19, 15, 0.6)`. Typography is `label-caps-wide` in `#D1CBC0`. Active filter states invert the scheme: background `#D4AF55`, text `#080807`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** 16px × 16px sharp squares. 1px border in `#4A463F`. When checked, the background fills with `#D4AF55` with an internal 2px inset negative space before an ivory dot/tick.
- **Radios:** 14px × 14px sharp rotated diamond or strict square selector (not round). Active state features a nested solid gold square `#D4AF55`.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **The Crucible Gauge:** Radial or horizontal precision tick-mark slider for karat selection (18K, 22K, 24K) with calibrated ticks rendered in `#3A3428` and the needle active head rendered in polished `#E4C979`.
- **Ledger Row:** Clean horizontal list items separated by 1px hairline rules in `#1A1814`, featuring left-aligned serif item names and right-aligned, monospaced purity, weight, and valuation calculations.
